See it work: one booking, start to finish.

This is the demo as you would see it on a call, one step at a time. A request comes in, the owner’s phone buzzes, one tap, and the visit is on the calendar.

A working demo built around a sample shop; I run it live on a call. It runs on my own calendar, email and phone. The shop, the customer, the times and the numbers are samples. None of this runs for a customer yet.

The demo, step by step

Step 1 of 6

A customer asks for a visit on the shop’s website.

They fill in a short request: name, phone, email, what is wrong, and when they would like someone to come. Nothing is booked yet.

Sample. Website request, Sample HVAC Co.

Name
Dana
Phone
(555) 010-0142
Email
dana@example.com
Problem
Furnace making a banging noise
When
Thursday morning
Step 2 of 6

The owner gets one message, with two buttons.

The calendar is checked first, so the message already says which time is open. It arrives on the owner’s phone in the Telegram app. Nothing is booked and nothing goes to the customer until someone taps Approve.

Try the buttons on the right. They only change this card.

Sample. Message to the owner, Sample HVAC Co.

From
Dana, (555) 010-0142
About
Furnace making a banging noise
Asked for
Thursday morning
Open time
Thursday, 9:30 am

Nothing is sent from this page.

Step 3 of 6

One tap, and the visit is on the calendar.

Before it books, it checks again that the time is still open. In the demo, the calendar entry starts with [DEMO] so nobody mistakes it for a real job.

Sample. Calendar, Sample HVAC Co.

Thursday

9:30 to 10:30 am · [DEMO] Dana, furnace noise(555) 010-0142

Booked after the owner tapped Approve.

Step 4 of 6

The customer gets a confirmation email that says it is automated.

The wording is the one the owner approved in writing, in English or Spanish. It goes out only Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 7 pm. In the demo, the confirmation goes to my own inbox, not to a customer.

Sample. Automated email, Sample HVAC Co.

To
dana@example.com
Subject
Your visit on Thursday at 9:30 am

This is an automated message from Sample HVAC Co.

Hi Dana, your visit is set for Thursday at 9:30 am. To change it, reply to this email or call (555) 010-0100.

Step 5 of 6

The day before, a reminder email.

If Dana replies, the reply goes to the owner. Nothing is answered for him. If she replies STOP, or PARAR, she is never emailed by it again.

Sample. Automated email, Sample HVAC Co.

To
dana@example.com
Subject
Reminder: your visit tomorrow

This is an automated reminder from Sample HVAC Co. Your visit is tomorrow, Thursday, at 9:30 am.

Reply STOP and we will not email you again.

Step 6 of 6

Monday morning, the week in one message.

One short summary goes to the owner only, never to a customer. It counts what the sample shop’s own records show.

Sample. Monday summary to the owner, Sample HVAC Co.

New requests
9
Visits booked
6
Reminders sent
6
Quotes still open
4

Sample numbers, not results.
