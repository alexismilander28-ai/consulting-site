CMRI Consulting

Wilkes-Barre area · English and Spanish

I find the office work in your shop that AI can handle, with a person still in charge.

I come myself, to shops and offices around Wilkes-Barre, Scranton and Hazleton.

Start with a free missed-call test

  1. You send me your business phone number or your website.
  2. I send one ordinary request, the kind a new customer sends, or I call once after hours. I use my real name.
  3. I write down when it went out and when a person first answered.
  4. You get the exact times in a private email.

One test only. No fake emergencies, no recorded calls, and no sales call unless you ask for one.

Then, if you want it, a visit

I sit with you and whoever answers the phone, for about two to three hours. I take notes and record nothing. Within two weeks I hand you a written report, written from what I saw and what you told me. It is yours to use with anyone.

The visit is paid. You get the cost in writing before you commit. You pick one person at your shop to be in charge of any automation. They approve what goes out and can switch it off. I train them.

I make no promise of jobs or money. The AI workers are a working demo built around a sample shop; I run it live on a call. None runs for a customer yet.
