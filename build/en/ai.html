I train them. It can be you, your office manager, or whoever already answers the phone.

What the AI Supervisor does

  • Approves the wording of every message before it goes out, in English and Spanish.
  • Taps Approve or Decline on each booking.
  • Reads the log of what was sent and answers the replies a person should answer.
  • Pauses everything with one switch if something looks wrong.

The rules every worker follows

  1. Every message to a customer comes from wording you approved in writing, in English and in Spanish.
  2. Every worker sends email only. None sends text messages.
  3. Messages go out only Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 7 pm in the customer’s local time (your shop’s, if the customer’s is not known). Never on a US federal holiday.
  4. A customer who asks to stop, in English or Spanish, is not contacted again.
  5. Anything the software says in an email or a chat is disclosed as automated. It never pretends to be a person.
  6. You get an alert if something fails, a switch that pauses everything at once, and a log of every message sent.
  7. The accounts are in your business’s name. I get access only through a separate access form that you sign, read-only to start, and you can remove it any day.
  8. I don’t take on health care, insurance, lending, collections, tax, accounting, legal or child care businesses.
