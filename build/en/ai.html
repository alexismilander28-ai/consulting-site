AI workers are software. Each one does one repetitive office job.

One texts back a missed call. Another sends a reminder the day before a visit. Every message says it is automated, and a person at your shop stays in charge.

Where this stands today.

  • They are built and tested on made-up data.
  • None is running for a customer yet.
  • The phone receptionist is not offered yet.
  • Building them and running them for a business starts after a separate company and a written agreement exist.

The rules every worker follows

  1. Every message to a customer comes from wording you approved in writing, in English and in Spanish.
  2. Messages go out only Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 7 pm Eastern. Never on Sundays or Pennsylvania holidays.
  3. A customer who says STOP, in English or Spanish, is not contacted again.
  4. Anything the software says in a text or a chat is disclosed as automated. It never pretends to be a person.
  5. You get an alert if something fails, a switch that pauses everything at once, and a log of every message sent.
  6. The accounts are in your business’s name. You choose who has access and you can remove anyone.
  7. For licensed professions it only schedules and passes messages along. It never quotes, advises or gives opinions.
