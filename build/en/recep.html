The receptionist: what I can show you, and what is not offered yet.

On a website, a chat assistant can answer the common questions and take a request for a visit. On the phone, nothing is offered yet.

Where this stands today.

  • The phone receptionist is not offered yet. Nothing on this site answers a phone call.
  • The website chat is a working demo built around a sample shop; I run it live on a call.
  • It is not on any customer’s website.

On a website: a chat with fixed answers

Its first line says it is automated and not a person. It answers from a short list of answers the owner wrote and approved ahead of time: hours, the area served, languages, and the next open times. It does not make up replies.

When a visitor wants a visit, it takes their name, phone, email and one of the open times, and passes the request to the owner. The owner approves it, the same way as in the booking demo.

Anything else gets one fixed sentence that sends the question to a person. That includes cost, complaints and refunds. It never states a cost.

Sample. Website chat, Sample HVAC Co.

AssistantHi. I’m the automated scheduling assistant for Sample HVAC Co. I’m not a person. I can answer common questions and take a request for a visit.

VisitorDo you come out to Kingston?

AssistantYes. Sample HVAC Co. serves Kingston and the Wilkes-Barre area, Monday to Saturday.

VisitorHow much is a furnace check?

AssistantA person at the office answers questions about cost. I can take a request for a visit and pass your question along.

VisitorOK. Thursday morning?

AssistantThe next open times are Thursday at 9:30 am or 1:00 pm. Which one, and what name and phone should I give the office? Nothing is booked until the office approves it.

Sample conversation. The shop, the times and the questions are samples.

By phone: not offered yet

I do not offer a phone receptionist today, and I have not installed one for anyone. When a call comes in to your shop, a person still answers it, or it goes to your voicemail as it does now.

If that ever changes, this page will say so first, along with how callers are told they are talking to an automated system.
