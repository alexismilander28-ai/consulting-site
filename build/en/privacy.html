What this policy covers

This policy covers this website, the free missed-call test, and people who contact me about my consulting work.

It does not cover the information inside a client’s own email, calendar or other systems. That is governed by the signed agreement between me and that client, and by the client’s own privacy notice. If you are a customer of one of my clients and have a question about your information, please ask that business. I will help them answer.

What I collect

What you send me. If you email, call or text me, I receive what you type or say: for example your name, email address, phone number, business name and message. Nothing on this site sends anything to me or to any server. It has no form that submits to a server. An email button opens your own email app with a short message to carlos@cmriconsulting.com already written. A text button opens your own messages app with a short message to (570) 392-9271 already written. Nothing leaves your device until you press send in that app. Email arrives in my business mailbox at Google Workspace. Calls and texts arrive on my business phone.

The free missed-call test. If you ask for it, you send me your business phone number or website. I send your business one ordinary request, the kind a new customer sends, or I call it once after hours, using my real name. I write down the time my request went out and the time a person first answered, and I send you those times in a private email. I do not record the call. I keep your times with your inquiry, for as long as the section “How long I keep it” says. I may also keep totals across many tests, with no business names, which I keep without a time limit.

Ordinary server logs. The company that hosts this site keeps standard logs of visits: IP address, date and time, the page asked for, and the browser type. I use these only to keep the site running and to look into problems.

Fonts. This site loads its fonts from Google. When your browser fetches them, Google can see your IP address.

What I do not collect. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. It has no accounts, and the site itself takes no payments. Invoices may be paid through Stripe, which handles card details. I do not buy lists of people’s information.

Do Not Track. Because this site does not track visitors, it treats every visit the same way, whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

What I use it for

  • To reply to you.
  • To run the free test you asked for and send you the result.
  • To set up and hold a call, demo or visit you asked for.
  • To prepare a proposal or agreement you asked for.
  • To keep business, tax and legal records.

I do not send marketing texts. If you text me, a person texts you back. I do not add you to an email list unless you ask.

Who I share it with

I do not sell your information. I do not share it for anyone else’s advertising.

I use a small number of service providers to run the business, and they handle information for me:

  • an email, document and video-call provider (Google Workspace, including Google Meet);
  • the website host (Render);
  • my phone service, for calls and texts;
  • an AI model provider (Anthropic), which I use to draft documents such as proposals and agreements. I send it only a business name, public information about your business such as your website, the names and work email addresses of the people a document is addressed to, and my own notes, which hold no other names. I do not send it your message text, your phone number, or the results of a free test;
  • a payment service (Stripe), if you pay one of my invoices through it. Stripe handles your card details, and you enter them on Stripe’s page only.

Each uses it under its own terms to provide its service. For Anthropic, I turned off the setting that lets it train on my content.

I may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect my legal rights.

AI tools

I use AI tools in my own work, for example to draft documents. The only AI tool your information goes into is the one named above (Anthropic), and only the items listed there. I use it under a paid account, and I have turned off the setting that would let it use my content to train its models. I do not use your information for anyone else.

How long I keep it

  • If you contact me, or ask for a free test, and do not become a client: up to 24 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • If you become a client: for the length of the work and 4 years after, or longer if the law requires.
  • Server logs: for as long as the host keeps them. I do not keep my own copy.

Your choices

Security

I protect my accounts with passwords and two-step verification. No method is perfect. If I learn that your information was exposed, I will tell you where the law requires it.

Changes
