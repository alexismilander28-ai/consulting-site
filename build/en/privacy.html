Privacy

CMRI Consulting is the business name of Carlos Alexis Milander, a sole proprietor in Pennsylvania. “I” and “me” here mean Carlos.

Short version. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. I do not sell your information. Ask me to delete it and I will. Contact: {{EMAIL}}.

What this covers

This website, and people who contact me about my consulting work. It does not cover the information inside a client’s own email, calendar or other systems. That is governed by the signed agreement between me and that client, and by the client’s own privacy notice. If you are a customer of one of my clients and have a question about your information, please ask that business. I will help them answer.

What I collect

What you send me. If you email, call or text me, I receive what you type or say: your name, email address, phone number, business name and message. The buttons on this site open your own email or messages app with a short note already written. Nothing is sent until you press send.

Ordinary server logs. The company that hosts this site keeps standard logs of visits: IP address, date and time, the page asked for, and the browser type. I use these only to keep the site running and to look into problems.

Fonts. This site loads its fonts from Google. When your browser fetches them, Google can see your IP address.

What I do not collect. No cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers. No accounts and no payments on this site. I do not buy lists of people’s information.

What I use it for

  • To reply to you.
  • To set up and hold a call or visit you asked for.
  • To prepare a proposal or agreement you asked for.
  • To keep business, tax and legal records.

I do not send marketing texts. If you text me, the reply comes from me, a person. I do not add you to an email list unless you ask.

Who I share it with

I do not sell your information, and I do not share it for anyone else’s advertising. A few service providers handle information for me to run the business: an email and document provider (Google Workspace), the website host (Render), my phone service, and, once you become a client, a payment processor and an e-signature service. They may use it only to provide their service to me. I may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect my legal rights.

AI tools

I use AI tools in my own work, for example to draft documents. I do not put your contact details or your message into an AI tool to train it, and I do not use them for anyone else.

How long I keep it

  • If you contact me and do not become a client: up to 24 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • If you become a client: for the length of the work and 4 years after, or longer if the law requires.
  • Server logs: as long as the host keeps them. I keep no copy of my own.

Your choices

You can ask what information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email {{EMAIL}}. I answer within 30 days. If the law requires me to keep some records, I will tell you. You can ask me to stop contacting you at any time, by any method, and I will.

Children

This site is for businesses. It is not directed to children, and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Security

I protect my accounts with passwords and two-step verification. No method is perfect. If I learn that your information was exposed, I will tell you where the law requires it.

Changes

If I change this policy, I will post the new version here with a new date. If the change is important, I will say so at the top of the page.
