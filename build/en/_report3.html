3. What I would change first, in this order

  1. Answer every missed call the same day: an email when the caller’s address is known, otherwise a call back from a person. In the sample, most unanswered calls come after 4:30 pm, when nobody is at the desk.
  2. Give every open quote a second and a third call, on set days. In the sample, a quote gets one follow-up at most.
  3. Send a reminder the day before each appointment. In the sample, none of the no-shows got one.
