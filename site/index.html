Three places the money leaks out

None of these show up on a profit and loss statement. They show up as jobs that went to someone else.

For small businesses in eastern Pennsylvania: contractors, home services, offices, stores, anyone whose work comes in by phone, text or email.

The examples on this page use a heating contractor. The same three leaks show up at a dental office, an insurance agency, a repair shop or a store that takes orders by phone.

  1. Tue 6:42 pmMissed call, no message

    The call nobody answered

    The crew is up on a roof, the office closed at 4:30, the phone rings out. A caller who reaches voicemail may not leave a message. They call the next business on the list.

  2. Mon 9:15 amQuote sent, no follow-up

    The quote nobody chased

    You drove out, measured, wrote it up and sent it. Then the week got busy. A customer comparing three quotes may go with whoever called back.

  3. Thu 9:30 pmInvoices, reminders, reviews

    The paperwork after dinner

    Appointment reminders, nudges on unpaid invoices, asking happy customers for a review, typing the same job into two systems. It gets done at the kitchen table, or it doesn't get done.

The rules every worker follows
  • Every message to a customer comes from wording you approved in writing, in English and in Spanish.
  • Messages go out only Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 7 pm Eastern, never on Sundays or Pennsylvania holidays.
  • A customer who says STOP, in English or Spanish, is not contacted again.
  • Anything AI says out loud or in chat is disclosed as automated. It never pretends to be a person.
  • You get an alert if something fails, a switch that pauses everything at once, and a log of every message sent.
  • It works for contractors and home services, barbershops and salons, auto repair, and independent insurance agents. For licensed professions it only schedules and relays messages. It never quotes, advises or gives opinions.
  • No promise of jobs, savings or results. Every figure in your Map is an estimate from your own numbers.

Next: Questions

Straight answers

Is this an AI product?

The Map is a person in your office and a written report. After that, yes: I build an AI team for your office. Most of it is plain automation, like a reminder about an open quote. Some of it uses AI, like the receptionist. The report says which, and says where a person should stay in charge.

What won't it do?

It won't pretend to be a person: an AI receptionist says it is automated. It won't send a customer anything you haven't approved. It won't replace the person who runs your office; it takes the repetitive part off their desk. And I won't promise you a number of jobs or dollars. Every dollar figure in the report is an estimate built from your own numbers.

Who approves what customers see?

In the Map, nothing reaches your customers. For anything built later, every automatic message is a template you approve in writing first, in English and Spanish. The AI receptionist answers within rules you set and hands off to a person for prices, complaints and refunds.

Do I have to switch software?

No. The Map works with whatever you use today, and I don't touch it. Most fixes start with getting the software you already pay for to work properly, not replacing it.

What about the rules on texting customers?

In the Map, nothing is sent to anyone. If texting is part of a fix you build later, it comes with rules on consent, opting out and carrier registration: the registration should be under your business, you keep the opt-in records, and STOP is tested before anything sends. I'm not a lawyer, so the final word on your message wording belongs to your attorney.

How much does it cost?

One fixed fee. I tell you the number on the first call, and it is written in a short agreement you read before you pay anything. Nothing is owed for the call or the free test. Software and texting fees, if you ever add them, are billed to you directly by those companies.

Do you work in Spanish?

Yes. The call, the visit, the interviews with your staff and the written report, in English, Spanish, or both.

What happens to my accounts?

Nothing of yours is held by me. The Map needs no access to your accounts. If you later hire anyone to build, everything stays in your accounts and you can remove their access.

Who sees my numbers?

Only me. What I learn in your office stays confidential and is used only for your report. I take written notes, record nothing, and never ask for passwords, card or bank numbers. I describe your business publicly only if you say yes in writing.

Where do you work?

I'm based in the Wilkes-Barre area and work across Eastern and Northeastern Pennsylvania. The Map includes time in your office, in person.
