# Art direction: Carlos Milander site        date: 2026-11-15     status: DRAFT for owner

Full brief: `research/ATTENTION-PLAN.md` section 2 (owner approved). This page records what was built.

- **Who is speaking.** Carlos Milander, one person. Wilkes-Barre, Scranton, Hazleton, Allentown area.
  English, and Spanish in usted written as its own pages under `site/es/`.
- **Offered today / not yet.** Today: the free missed-call test and the Map. Not yet: AI workers
  (built and tested on made-up data, none running for a customer, phone receptionist not offered,
  Build and Run after a separate company and a written agreement exist).
- **Hard rules.** No prices. No guarantees or promised results. No fake proof. AI always disclosed
  as automated. Every sample labeled made-up. Name is "Carlos Milander" only.
- **Material world.** A printed report, a pen's red marks, a phone screenshot, a photo print.
- **The one bold move.** The sample report page as a sheet of paper in the first screen, tilted two
  degrees, with his photo laid on it. It is built so one real photo can replace it (see the comment
  in `build/en/home.html`).
- **Three things removed.** The six-card menu. The drawn path illustration. The nine-tab bar with
  the theme and language switches.
- **Type.** Source Serif 4 for headline and body (one weight, 400). Source Sans 3 for header,
  buttons, captions and the report's tables. Body 19 px desktop, 17 px phone. Headline 52 / 32 px.
- **Colour.** Ground #F6F3EC, band #ECE7DA, ink #1C1A17, accent #1D3F9C (pressable things only),
  signal #B4281E (marks on the sample report, one missed-call line in the demo).
- **Shape.** Radii: 6 px buttons, 3 px photos (14 px only inside the phone-screenshot bubbles).
  One shadow. Paper is square. One tilted object. No motion beyond button press.
- **Census on the render (home, 1280):** 2 radii, 1 shadow, 2 families, 1 filled button, 4 nav links.
