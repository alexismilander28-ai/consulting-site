## Why ours reads as AI-made

1. The home page is a menu: six identical icon cards (the most common AI-landing-page pattern).
2. No photo of a person or place on the home page (1 image, the headshot, inside About; 87 inline SVGs).
3. The hero art is a tidy, symmetric abstract illustration that looks generated.
4. Headline uses the light-then-bold serif emphasis template habit.
5. Dark navy with a pale-blue button is the category default.
6. Copy runs in threes with check icons.
7. Navigation feels like an app: about 10 tabs plus theme and language toggles; the tab row scrolls off the edge on a phone.
8. The proof is hidden: "based in the Wilkes-Barre area" and "I work alone" sit in the About tab.
9. "Made-up" labels are honest but nothing real sits beside them.
10. Small light-grey text on dark, evenly weighted; no real-artifact texture.

## What to change first

No clients, testimonials or work photos exist yet, so none of these fake them. No prices, no guarantees, AI always labeled as automated.

1. Put Carlos in the hero in a real place (candid photo at a counter or desk with the sample report). One line in his voice: "I'm Carlos. I live in the Wilkes-Barre area and I come to your shop myself." Move "I work alone" to the home page.
2. Replace the six-card grid with the sample Map itself: 2-3 printed-looking report pages, captioned "Sample report for a made-up HVAC shop."
3. Add a playable demo recording of the missed-call text-back (30-60 s), labeled "Demo on a test number. The texts are automated and say so." English and Spanish.
4. Cut the nav to four links (the Map, AI workers, About, Contact) with the phone number as text in the header. Nothing scrolls sideways on a phone.
5. Go light and plain by default: warm off-white, one serif weight, short paragraphs, a "How a visit goes" section in his own words naming real towns. Write the Spanish page as its own text.

Do not copy: invented KPI tiles (01), "Guarantee" (05), coupon prices (09), ratings and logos (10), outcome claims (04).
