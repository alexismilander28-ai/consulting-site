## Verdict

From the screenshots: the first thing the eye lands on is a sheet of paper with red pencil marks
and his photo laid on it, then one sentence in his voice, then one blue button. A stranger can
answer who (Carlos Milander), where (Wilkes-Barre area), what (he visits and writes a report), and
how to reach him (the number is in the header as text). The paper sheet is rendered, not
photographed, so the page is honest but not yet as strong as it will be with the real photo.

Every page is self-contained (inline CSS, JS and photo). Sizes: 27 to 67 KB each (the old single
file was 294 KB). Pages are generated by `python build/build.py` from `build/`.

**Headline used.** "I'm Carlos. I come to your shop and write down where calls and quotes go
unanswered." Beside the button: "I'll contact your business the way a new customer would, and tell
you how long the answer took." Spanish: "Soy Carlos. Voy a su negocio y anoto dónde se quedan sin
respuesta las llamadas y los presupuestos."

**Contact details** live in one place: the `CONTACT` block at the top of `build/build.py`. Change
the phone or email there, run the build, and all ten pages follow.

**Main button.** On a phone it opens a ready-written text to (570) 392-9271. On a computer it opens
a ready-written email. This is done with a CSS media query, so it works without JavaScript.

**Swapping in the real photo.** In `build/en/home.html` and `build/es/home.html`, replace the one
`<div class="hero-art">` block with one `<img class="hero-photo">`. The comment there says how.

## What was proven

- Served with the production policy from `render.yaml` (`serve_csp.py --csp-from`): console clean
  on all 10 pages at 375, 768 and 1280. Only the two Google font hosts are contacted.
- No horizontal overflow at any width; header links all on screen at 375 in both languages.
- Heights: home 1288 px (EN) and 1339 px (ES) at 1280, which is 1.6 to 1.7 screens (target was
  about 1.5). Home at 375: 2004 px (EN), 2183 px (ES), inside the plan's 3200 limit.
- Main button in the first phone screen at 375x812 and 390x844 in both languages. On a small
  360x740 phone it is just below.
- Keyboard: every link, button and summary takes Tab focus with a visible 3 px ring. Report pager,
  one-at-a-time worker list and Approve / Decline sample work. With JavaScript off, all three
  report pages show and nothing is hidden.
- axe (WCAG AA) through `eyes.py`: 0 violations on all 10 pages.
- Leave-behind prints on exactly one US Letter page in both languages (PDF made and counted).
- Compliance search on the built files, both languages: no prices or currency, no guarantee words,
  no result claims, no "fix it", no "AI team", no testimonial words, no banned names, no dashes used
  as joiners, no external URLs except the font hosts, no forms, iframes, cookies or storage.
  Required status lines and "automated" first lines are present in both languages.
- Spanish pages: no string shared with the English pages except names, numbers and the made-up
  shop name; no English words found in Spanish text.

## Not verified

- Real phones and real browsers other than headless Chromium. The text-message link (`sms:` with a
  ready-written body) was checked as a link only; whether each phone opens it with the text filled
  in was not tested.
- The Spanish. It was written as its own text, in usted, but the owner has not read it yet.
- The live host. Nothing was deployed, so Render's own headers and paths were not exercised.
- A stranger's reaction, a screen reader, and print on a real printer.
- `eyes.py` reports one console error per page that is its own: axe tries to fetch the font
  stylesheet and the policy blocks that fetch. The page itself logs nothing.
